Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026.

Supperwise collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of its own.

What you enter (your household, diets and allergies, ZIP code, store, budget, plans, grocery and pantry ticks) is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or using Settings › Erase all my data, removes it.

Subscriptions are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we don't receive your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

We use RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and an anonymous ID it creates, so your subscription works across devices and restores correctly. It doesn't receive your name, email or the meals you plan. RevenueCat's privacy policy applies to that data.

If you turn on the dinner reminder, notifications are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS.

Product scanner. When you scan or type a barcode, the app sends only that barcode number to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to look up the product. No account or personal information is sent. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy applies to that request.

Saving a recipe from a link. The app opens the link you paste, the same way a browser would, and reads the recipe on your iPhone.

Camera and photos. The camera reads barcodes on your iPhone. Receipt and fridge photos you choose are read on your iPhone by Apple's built-in text and image recognition and are never uploaded.

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
