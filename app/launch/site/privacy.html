Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026.

The short version: your meal plans, household, diets and allergies stay on your iPhone. You only need an account to share recipes or use the food scan, and then we keep just your email and what you share. No ads, no tracking, no selling data.

Stored only on your iPhone

Your household (names, diets, allergies), ZIP code, store, budget, weekly plans, lunches, baby's first-foods log, grocery and pantry ticks, saved recipes and restaurant dishes are stored only in the app on your iPhone. They aren't sent to us. Deleting the app, or Settings › Erase all my data, removes them.

Account (optional)

To share recipes or scan food you sign in with your email address and a one-time code. We store your email and an account ID with our backend provider, Supabase (supabase.com), which hosts our database and server functions. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings › Account, or Community › My shared recipes › Delete my account). That deletes your account, your shared recipes and their photos.

Community recipes

When you share a recipe, we store the recipe, the first name you give, and the photo you choose. It stays private until a Supperwise reviewer approves it; then other people using the app can see it with your first name. If you report a recipe, we store your report and the reason you give.

Food scan

When you scan food, the photo is sent to our server and analysed by Anthropic's Claude AI (anthropic.com) to estimate what the food is and its nutrition. The app asks your permission before the first scan. Only the photo is sent: not your name, plan or household. We don't store the photo. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms, which don't allow using it to train their models, and may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring. We count how many scans each account makes each month.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we don't receive your payment details (apple.com/legal/privacy). We use RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) to manage subscriptions: it receives your purchase history and an anonymous ID so your subscription works across devices and restores correctly.

Other lookups

Barcodes: the app sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to look up the product. Recipe links: the app opens the link you paste, as a browser would. Receipts and fridge photos: read on your iPhone by Apple's built-in recognition and never uploaded. Dinner reminders: scheduled on your iPhone by iOS.

Children

Supperwise is for adults planning family meals and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.
