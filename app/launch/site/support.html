How are prices worked out?

Each ingredient starts from a national reference price for one pack, adjusted for your kind of store and your region. They're estimates: shelf prices change and differ by branch, so your total at the register will differ. Supperwise isn't affiliated with any store.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Supperwise › Cancel. Or in the app: Settings › Manage or cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

I subscribed on another iPhone

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

A dish had something we can't eat

Check each person's settings under Next week › Who's eating. Dishes with an allergen someone avoids are left out, but always check ingredient labels, and tell us which dish so we can fix it.
