# Supperwise press kit
**What it is:** Supperwise is an iPhone app that plans a week of dinners for a household and prices them as one grocery list, by the whole pack you buy, for your kind of store and region. It applies each person's diet, allergies and spice level, and writes a vegetarian plate into meat dishes.
