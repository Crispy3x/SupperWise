# Supperwise: what to add next (2026-11-12)

Each idea is scored on four things:
- **Keeps people paying:** does it bring them back weekly or daily, or build up data they won't want to lose?
- **Cheap to run:** does it work on the phone, or need a server or AI?
- **Grows downloads:** is it shareable, searchable, or does it reach a community?
- **Effort:** how much it takes to build.

## My order
1. **Siri, widgets, Apple Watch (#6) and the shared family list (#10).** Low effort, and both keep the app in front of people every day. The shared list is already promised in the plans.
2. **Baby and toddler mode (#1).** In the market scan, "starting solids" had weak competition and a daily, months-long need. Inside Supperwise it's a strong reason to pick Family.
3. **Lunchbox planner (#2).** Turns a dinner app into a school-day app, using the leftovers feature we just built.
4. **Holiday menus (#7): Thanksgiving is built, in Plus and up** (subscriptions only, no one-time purchases). Each holiday becomes a reason to subscribe, and the 7-day trial covers the day. Christmas is next.
5. **Grocery delivery hand-off (#8).** Referral income on top of subscriptions, once a partner approves.

## Avoid for now
- **Store sales (#9):** needs data deals.
- **TikTok and Instagram saving (#11):** a cost on every use; wait until revenue covers it.
- **Calorie counting and macro logging as a main feature:** that's a crowded market with giants (MyFitnessPal, Cal AI). Our score and nutrition goals help people choose without turning the app into a food diary.
