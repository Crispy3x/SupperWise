## Listing text

mise – meal planner
Take the stress out of deciding what to eat.

mise helps you generate personalised meal plans in seconds based on your dietary preferences, calorie goals, favourite UK supermarkets, budget, and the ingredients you already have at home.

Whether you're meal prepping for the week, trying to eat healthier, save money on groceries, or simply reduce food waste, mise creates practical meal plans and shopping lists tailored to you.

Features
• Generate personalised weekly meal plans
• Build meals around ingredients already in your kitchen
• Create automatic shopping lists
• Choose from popular UK supermarkets
• Filter by dietary requirements and preferences
• Discover new recipes and meal ideas
• Save time planning meals each week
• Reduce food waste and unnecessary spending
