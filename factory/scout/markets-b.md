## 3. Excluded and why

- **Newspapers, magazines and financial research** (most of news; MarketWatch, Seeking Alpha, IBD, MarketSurge, Zacks, Fundstrat, Moby, Pour Over): need a journalism or content catalogue.
- **Social and creator platforms** (X, Reddit, Clubhouse, Substack, Medium, LinkedIn, WhatsApp Business, 2nd1st, Narwhal, Whop, Stan, SideShift, Love8, SayHi): need an existing network, or depend on a platform's API.
- **Music and audio streaming, live concerts, radio networks** (YouTube Music, Pandora, Amazon Music, SoundCloud, TIDAL, SiriusXM, iHeart, Deezer, Qobuz, Audiomack, Anghami, LiveOne, nugs, LivePhish, Play Dead, Fit Radio, 猫耳FM, Coast to Coast AM): need a licensed catalogue.
- **Tabs, sheet music, karaoke, song-based lessons** (Ultimate Guitar, Songsterr, MuseScore, Smule, Simply Sing, KaraFun, StarMaker, Jamzone, Piano apps, Worship Backing Tracks, Appcompanist, Whoop Triggerz, Loops by CDub): need licensed song content.
- **Music distribution and creator analytics** (DistroKid, UnitedMasters, Songstats, stats.fm, [untitled], BandLab): run on platform or rights infrastructure, or are a network.
- **Faith audio and video content** (Hallow, Abide, Dwell, Glorify, Bible BFF, Bible Voice, Sutara, Logos, Ascension, Life Bible): need a content catalogue or publisher rights.
- **Brokerage, crypto, lending, banking, money movement** (Webull, Kraken, Uphold, dub, Breakout, Trade Echo execution, Lenme, Till, Kiwipop, Ditch, Blossom, Delta, Solo's guaranteed pay): need a licence.
- **Credit scores and credit repair** (Experian, Credit Sesame, WalletHub credit, Dovly, Credit Booster AI **OD**, Dumbo Credit **OD**): need credit-bureau data contracts, and credit repair is regulated.
- **Identity protection and antivirus** (Aura, LifeLock, Norton, McAfee, Malwarebytes, TotalAV, AT&T ActiveArmor): big brands with threat and breach databases.
- **VPNs** (NordVPN, ExpressVPN, Proton, Mullvad, IPVanish, Free VPN, World VPN): need a server network, and trust brands dominate.
- **Spam-call blocking and caller ID** (Robokiller, Truecaller, Mr. Number, TrapCall): need a large call database or user network.
- **People search and genealogy** (Whitepages, BeenVerified, Intelius, TruthFinder, Ancestry, MyHeritage, Heritage): need data-broker or records catalogues (with FCRA rules) or a DNA lab.
- **Hardware companions** (Blink, Camtro **OD**, Seeing, Gazing **OD**, Kemo, VicoHome, Ubox, Sonos, FIXD, Print Master, La Crosse, Ambient Weather, Cricut, Brother Artspira, Zelus WBGT): need the device.
- **Marketplaces and B2B team SaaS** (Booksy, Backstage, Actors Access, Trucker Path, Swoopa, Mystro, Homebase, HotSchedules, Jobber, HoneyBook, Calendly, Zoom, Quo, Voxer, Manychat, GoDaddy, Boards, Sales Navigator, Jotform, QuickBooks, Wave, Splitwise): two-sided, network-dependent, or a heavy team product.
- **Games and game companions** (Pokémon HOME, Poke Genie, GO Raid Party, Beat Maker Pro, Day Trading Simulator, WBuilds, CarDex, Bitcoin "mining" apps, which are also dubious): excluded as games, or as scams.
- **Big-brand tools** (Adobe Acrobat, Scan, Express and Firefly; Google Flow; Kling; Dreamina; Procreate; ibis Paint; FlipaClip; DuckDuckGo; Merriam-Webster; Oxford; TWC; AccuWeather; FOX Weather; ForeFlight; Garmin Pilot): their brand or proprietary engine is the product. They are still counted as demand inside the markets above.
- **Professional and medical references and specialist data** (EMRA Antibiotic Guide, Cat DB, State Statute, LogTen, Case Tracker USCIS, Construction Master): medical-adjacent or proprietary data, and single apps anyway.
