## 3. Excluded and why

- **Dating apps** (most of lifestyle #1–#47 and social, including Tinder, Hinge, Grindr, the Black Cap open doors Christian Mingle/Jdate): two-sided network, and the brief excludes them.
- **Messaging and social networks** (Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, Snapchat, Instagram, Locket, sendit, NGL, Wizz, Yubo, Meetup, Timeleft, BRB, Chatty): need a large network / social graph.
- **Live streaming and video platforms** (YouTube, Twitch, KICK, BIGO, Rumble, LiveMe, voice-party rooms): streaming, creator marketplace.
- **Second phone number / free calling** (TextNow, Text Me, Text Free, Talkatone, Dingtone, TalkU, SpoofCard, Second Texting Number): telecom infrastructure and carrier regulation.
- **Instagram follower trackers, reposters, TikTok downloaders** (social #35, #36, #38, #39, #83, #94, #95): they depend on scraping other platforms against their terms, so one API change kills them.
- **Inmate messaging** (social #50 GTL, lifestyle #43 CorrLinks): need contracts with correctional systems.
- **Hardware companions** (Wyze, Arlo, Tapo, eufy, Nanit, Owlet, eero, ecobee, Tile, Hatch, Peloton, Zwift, iFIT, Withings, Pulsetto, Hume, Fitbit, GoPro, Insta360, Frameo, Nixplay, Skylight, Phomemo, Petlibro, BACtrack, Eko, Plaud, Pocket): need hardware.
- **Licensed/regulated medical** (FDA-cleared EpiWatch, Natural Cycles, EndeavorOTC, PMcardio, ECG readers, iPacemaker; AI medical scribes Freed, Chartnote, Commure, Conveyor, Moments, which need HIPAA BAAs and clinic sales; camera BP/glucose/temperature "measurement"): licence or medical-device claims.
- **Clinical references and anatomy atlases** (epocrates, Lexidrug, Davis's, Sanford, Johns Hopkins, 5MCC, AMBOSS, VisualDx, UpToDate, NYSORA, Vargo, Complete Anatomy, Visible Body): licensed medical content catalogues.
- **Content catalogues by named people / libraries** (Calm, Headspace, Insight Timer, Waking Up, BetterSleep, Breethe; trainer apps LADDER, Sweat, Alive, STNDRD, CGX, MWH, Sculpt Society, Evlo, FORM; MasterClass, Coursera, Udemy, Great Courses, Mindvalley; Pimsleur, Babbel, Busuu; Simply Piano/Guitar, Yousician, flowkey; Epic, Lingokids, Reading Eggs; CHANI, Core of the Heart): need a content catalogue or named talent.
- **Kids' games and games** (Toca Boca, Sago Mini, Kiddopia, Bebi, Chibi Dolls, KidloLand, ATLAS:EARTH, PokeRaid, PokerBROS, Warhammer): games, and PokerBROS is close to gambling.
- **Marketplaces** (Care.com, Roomster, Purple Ocean and Nebula psychic readers, HelloTalk exchange, Goldie, Casting Networks, Linktree, Zeely ads, WeWard rewards): two-sided markets.
- **Big-brand / first-party apps** (Google, Microsoft, Adobe, Amazon, Dropbox, Meta, OpenAI, Anthropic, X/Grok, Perplexity, Canva, CapCut, AARP, AT&T, Duolingo, ClassDojo): big-company products. They count as charting apps for a job but not as entry evidence.
- **VPNs and password managers** (Super Unlimited Proxy, Surfshark, X-VPN, Norton, Hotspot Shield, Guru; Keeper, 1Password, Dashlane, LastPass, Proton Mail): server fleets and security trust. One person can technically run a VPN, but trust and abuse handling make it a poor fit.
- **Co-parenting communication** (AppClose, TalkingParents, OurFamilyWizard): court-record features and a two-party network. Three apps chart, but there's no open door and it's legally sensitive.
- **Car coding** (BimmerCode): needs an OBD hardware adapter.
