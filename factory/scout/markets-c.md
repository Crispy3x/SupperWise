## 3. Left out, and why

- **Video streaming (Netflix, Peacock, Max, Disney+, sports networks, FOX One, CHSN, etc.)**: content catalogue and rights.
- **Short-drama apps (DramaBox, ReelShort, VibeShort, BlinkDrama and ~25 more; many open doors)**: content catalogue — the open doors are production studios, not app builders.
- **Web-novel, manga, comics, audiobook and ebook apps (GoodNovel, Audible, Webtoon, NovelMist etc.)**: content catalogue / licensed books.
- **Publisher recipe apps (NYT Cooking, America's Test Kitchen, Bon Appétit, Delish, Tasty, Guardian Feast, Provecho, Scoolinary, Gronda)**: content catalogue / courses by named chefs.
- **Kids' story/audio content (Moshi, Vooks, Novel Effect, Minno, Dipsea, Quinn)**: content catalogue.
- **Marketplaces (OfferUp, Carousell, Haraj, Bazaraki, Marketplace, ResX exchange, Cameo, Raide rentals, Couchsurfing, misterb&b, Hostelworld, Harvest Hosts, Momma sitters)**: two-sided marketplace.
- **Big-brand companions (My Disney Experience, Disneyland, Amazon, Williams Sonoma, NFL, NBA, MLB, WNBA, NASCAR, UFC, Thermomix, Anova, Garmin, Navico, Minn Kota, Cardo, Blast, Oceanic+, Topps card traders, D&D Beyond)**: brand, league or hardware tie-in.
- **Hardware-dependent (NOBL AIR luggage tracker, OBD Fusion, launch-monitor golf sim, Tractor GPS for machinery)**: needs hardware.
- **Sports betting research / pick apps (Linemate, Outlier, Props.Cash, Action Network, PickFinder, Rithmm, PropGPT, HOF, BettingPros, Elite Picks, PropsMadness, Pikkit) and PPPoker**: betting / real-money gambling adjacent — excluded per the rules, even though the research tools themselves need no licence.
- **Fintech (Bridge debit rewards) and legal-membership (Auto Allies)**: licence / regulated.
- **Social networks and network-effect apps (TikTok, Patreon, Beli, Fable social, nomadtable, tripbff, FlyMeOut, Wetolove, Real, Exit51, GameChanger, TeamSnap, DUPR, UTR, Geocaching, Untappd community, Hudl)**: needs an existing network / social graph or B2B org sales.
- **Games (WePlay, Picolo, Color by Number, Bluey, Claw Eden prize mall, TREAT)**: games excluded.
- **Live data networks (Transit, Moovit, MarineTraffic, LandGlide/LandMap parcel data, Drivewyze, FerryFriend, StaffTraveler/Staff Airlines non-rev loads)**: depend on large proprietary data or agency integrations.
- **Professional niches (Wellsite Navigator, Petro Path, PatrolNav, PermitPass, LeadNav, MilGPS, CamoAg, Flamingo-style B2B compliance suites)**: tiny B2B audiences; possible but not this playbook.
- **Free-stuff / garage-sale aggregators (Snag zero-dollar, Freebie Alerts, Yardly, Yard Sale Treasure Map)**: depend on scraping or user-posted listings (marketplace-like).
