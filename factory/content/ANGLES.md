# Angles and hooks · Supperwise
Hooks are slide one: 12 words or fewer, lowercase, about the viewer's problem, never about the app.
The problems come from competitors' reviews (factory/SPEC.md), written in our own words.

## pain: the moment it bites
1. 6pm, fridge open, four people asking what's for dinner
2. me at the register finding out what "budget week" actually cost
3. cooking three different dinners because nobody eats the same thing
4. the meal plan said $80. the receipt said otherwise
5. day 4 of the meal plan and it's chicken again
6. when the "free trial" charged you before you saw a single recipe

## curiosity: something worth knowing
1. the recipe needs one teaspoon. the store sells you the whole jar
2. why your grocery budget is always off by about twenty dollars
3. the cheapest dinner of the week wasn't the one i expected
4. most meal plans price the spoonful, not the pack
5. one vegetarian at the table changes the whole grocery list
6. the most expensive thing in my cart was a spice i used once

## list: short and saveable
1. 5 weeknight dinners that share half their ingredients
2. 4 things i check before i trust a meal plan app
3. dinners that work when one kid won't eat anything spicy
4. 5 pantry staples that quietly fix your grocery bill
5. what a $95 week of family dinners can look like
6. 3 rules for planning dinner with a vegetarian in the house

## story: a small scene with a turn
1. text thread: "what's for dinner" / "i have a plan" / the plan
2. text thread: partner asks why there's a third jar of cumin
3. text thread: "can you grab stuff for tacos" turns into a full list
4. text thread: the vegetarian sister is coming for the week
5. text thread: "we went over budget again"
6. text thread: kid asks if tonight is "the spicy one"

## demo: the app doing its one thing (in words until captures exist)
1. watch what happens when you type in 4 people and $95
2. you tell it who eats what. it plans around everyone
3. swap one dinner and watch the grocery total change
4. tick what's already in your cupboard. the bill drops
5. the whole week, priced before you leave the house
6. it shows you the plan before it asks for a cent
