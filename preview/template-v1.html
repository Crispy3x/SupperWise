Interactive prototype

Supperwise

A clickable preview of the iPhone app, running the real recipe catalog and the same rules as the Swift code: each person's diet and allergies, the kitchen you have, and grocery prices worked out by the whole pack you buy.

Try this

  1. Enter a ZIP code, add people (try a vegetarian and "No shellfish"), and build your first week.
  2. On the first week, tap Keep my free week.
  3. On Week, tap Swap: the plate spins and the price changes.
  4. Tap Start cooking, then I cooked it. The next night takes its own colour.
  5. In Groceries, tick items off. In Kitchen, tick what you have and watch the total drop.
  6. Tap Next week to change the budget, nights or household, and plan again.

What's different from the real app

Purchases are simulated here. Prices are the app's estimates for a supermarket in your region, not live store prices. Food photos are stand-ins until the app has its own photography. Nothing you enter leaves this page.
