## 2. Complaint themes by product

### Housecall Pro
1. Cancellation friction / lock-in: reports you must go through support chat and a call to cancel; a Capterra reviewer said the company "wants to lock you in on monthly contract". [S-weak aggregate; BBB: https://prod.bbb.org/us/ca/san-diego/profile/marketing-software/housecall-pro-1126-1000067843/complaints ; Capterra https://www.capterra.com/p/140363/HouseCall-Pro/reviews/?page=3]
2. Billing/refund disputes: BBB complainant reported payments "automatically refunded without my business' permission"; another said they lost $1,600. BBB shows 64 complaints in 3 years, not accredited. [S: BBB link above]
3. Narrow refunds, nonrefundable setup, unused credits forfeited. [S-weak: joinchargeback / fieldcamp pages]
4. Nickel-and-dime add-ons (SMS, extra locations, processing). [S-weak]
5. Mobile app: crashes/slowness on Android, GPS inaccuracy, weak offline per one competitor review; iOS reportedly better. [S-weak: pooldial.com, connecteam.com/reviews/housecall-pro/]; Trustpilot ~3.0/5 (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/housecallpro.com?page=2).

### ServiceTitan
1. Cost and opaque pricing. [S-weak: https://www.getonecrew.com/post/servicetitan-reviews , https://projul.com/blog/servicetitan-pricing-analysis-2026/]
2. Expensive, slow implementation (reports of paying for a year without onboarding in BBB complaints). [S-weak]
3. 12-month contract and cancellation difficulty; one customer said they cancelled in writing 30+ days ahead and still had trouble. [S-weak]
4. Support: long waits, rotating success managers. [S-weak]
5. Rating split: G2 4.5/5 (369 reviews) vs BBB ~1/5 (~30 reviews, 26 complaints). [S-weak] Likely irrelevant to small snow/landscape crews. [I]

### Square (Appointments / Invoices)
1. Frozen/held funds with no clear appeal; one 2025 BBB complaint: balance "shows $0" after promised 24-hour review; older reports of $20k held. [S: BBB https://prod.bbb.org/us/ca/san-francisco/profile/credit-card-merchant-services/square-inc-1116-370609/complaints?page=4 ; ITPro https://www.itpro.com/policy-legislation/compliance/356213/square-is-withholding-up-to-30-of-funds-and-small-businesses ; CBS https://www.cbsnews.com/dfw/news/business-owner-issue-square/ (older)]
2. Higher invoice rate (3.3% + 30c) on free tier vs card-present. [S: Square help]
3. Not a field-service tool (my inference: no routing/crew dispatch; not verified in this pass). [I]

### Workiz
1. Billing surprises, annual contracts, hard cancellation (Trustpilot ~3.1 across 126 reviews). [S-weak: https://www.rfp.wiki/crm-marketing/field-service-management/workiz]
2. Support unreachable: a May 2025 1.0 Capterra review said calling support 71 times. [S: https://www.capterra.com/p/147525/Workiz/reviews/2067482/ (as surfaced by search; exact review id match not confirmed)]
3. Add-ons (phone, AI answering) priced separately. [S-weak]
4. Payment-processing/account-management problems per GetApp summary. [S-weak: https://www.getapp.com/website-ecommerce-software/a/workiz/]

### Service Fusion
1. Support: long chat queues; refund promised then refused (two reviews). [S-weak: https://www.softwareadvice.com/field-service/service-fusion-profile/ ; https://www.lawnstarter.com/blog/reviews/software/service-fusion/]
2. No free trial, annual discount billed upfront. [S-weak]
3. Pricing sources disagree ($195-$259+). Otherwise ratings ~4.3 and flat unlimited-user pricing is viewed as a plus.

### FieldPulse
1. Sync/connectivity: Capterra theme ~85% negative; lag and offline issues; QuickBooks duplicates/failed syncs. [S-weak: https://www.capterra.com/p/153475/FieldPulse/reviews/]
2. Frequent bugs/glitches (~44% negative on that theme); overlapping ways to do a task, none fully working. [S-weak, same]
3. Pricing hard to pin down; costs rise with users/add-ons; processing fees sometimes higher. [S-weak: https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/fieldpulse (competitor)]

### Service Autopilot (Xplor)
1. Price increases ("another 10% increase, yet the features are not improving" per a SoftwareConnect reviewer; blog claims 25%+). [S-weak: https://softwareconnect.com/field-service-management/service-autopilot]
2. Declining support, slow feature development (blog) and a May 2025 1.0 Capterra "Poor support and value". [S-weak: https://www.capterra.com/p/122075/Service-Autopilot/reviews?page=2 ; https://crewscalepro.pages.dev/software/service-autopilot-review]
3. Forced in-house payment processing after ownership change (unsourced blogs; unconfirmed; I could not verify the acquirer). [S-weak]
4. Sign-up fee and ease-of-use 3.8/5. [S]

### Aspire
1. Not easy to use, overwhelming at first. [S-weak: Jobber's own roundup https://www.getjobber.com/academy/landscaping/aspire-landscape-software-alternatives/ (competitor); Capterra https://www.capterra.com/p/161544/Aspire/reviews/?page=5]
2. Implementation complicated; no onsite implementer. [S: Capterra via search]
3. More expensive/complex than LMN per a former LMN user. [S: Capterra]
4. Users praise support; many switched from LMN or Service Autopilot seeking job-costing/reporting. [S]

### LMN
1. Steep learning curve, "outdated" feel, takes time to learn. [S: Capterra https://www.capterra.com/p/142064/LMN/reviews/?page=3 ; Software Advice https://www.softwareadvice.com/landscaping/lmn-profile/reviews]
2. Crew time-clock adoption (foremen clocking right tasks). [S]
3. Limited estimate/contract formatting; QuickBooks cost-code integration gaps; time-app update glitches. [S]
4. Mostly positive (~89% positive on Capterra snapshot); users say they "maxed out" LMN and moved to Aspire. [S]

### GorillaDesk
1. Cost grows quickly with seats/routes; not affordable for larger operations. [S-weak: https://www.getapp.com/industries-software/a/gorilladesk/reviews/]
2. Integration drops (one reviewer). Few other complaints; 4.8 rating, praised support. [S-weak]

### Yardbook
1. No/unfinished iPhone app (beta for years per G2 reviewer; LawnStarter says no iOS app). [S: https://www.g2.com/products/Yardbook/reviews ; https://www.lawnstarter.com/blog/?p=52615]
2. Dated UI; hard to scale past a few hundred customers; no dispatch, no QuickBooks. [S]
3. Liked for being free and industry-specific. Tiny sample sizes (5-14 reviews).

### Kickserv
1. Mobile limitations, calendar/scheduling issues; app crashes after updates (low-quality aggregator). [S-weak: https://tooleduppro.pages.dev/reviews/kickserv ; Capterra https://capterra.com/p/122541/Kickserv/reviews/?page=6]
2. Customer service complaints in at least one Capterra review. Overall ratings 4.4-4.5. [S]

## 3. Cross-product patterns
- Billing/contract/cancellation friction (HCP, Workiz, ServiceTitan, SA, Service Fusion): the most common theme in BBB/Trustpilot-type sources. [S-weak, consistent]
- Price creep and add-on stacking (HCP SMS/locations, SA increases, GorillaDesk seats, FieldPulse users, Workiz phone). [S-weak]
- Payments: Square fund holds; forced/proprietary processing at SA; processing markups as hidden revenue. [S/S-weak]
- Support: reachability varies; slow queues at Workiz, Service Fusion; declining at SA. Small vendors (GorillaDesk, LMN, Aspire) praised on support. [S-weak]
- Learning curve/implementation for landscape-specific tools (LMN, Aspire) and enterprise (ServiceTitan). [S]
- Mobile reliability: Android crashes (HCP), sync/offline (FieldPulse), no iOS app (Yardbook), post-update glitches (LMN, Kickserv). [S-weak]
- Not found: a clear snow-specific complaint set (storm-mode dispatch, per-push/seasonal contracts, triggering by snowfall). Snow-removal results were vendor listicles only. [S + I]

## 4. What users like
Ease of use and quick scheduling/dispatch (FieldPulse, HCP), flat unlimited-user pricing (Service Fusion), responsive support and training (LMN, Aspire, GorillaDesk), job-costing confidence (LMN/Aspire), client portal, QuickBooks integration, free price (Yardbook). [S]

## 5. Gaps for an Apple-native, fairly priced all-in-one (mostly inference)
- Transparent flat pricing, no contract, one-click cancel, export-your-data guarantee; no SMS/location add-on nickel-and-diming. [I, backed by themes above]
- Payments done plainly: published rates, no forced processor, clear hold policy and fast payouts. [I]
- Offline-first native iOS/iPadOS/macOS app with reliable sync (counter to FieldPulse sync, HCP Android, Yardbook no-iOS). [I]
- Snow-specific: storm-triggered dispatch, per-event/seasonal/per-push billing, route re-optimization, proof-of-service photos/GPS time stamps, built-in time clock. Incumbents' snow coverage looks bolted-on (vendor listicles stress dispatch as where projects break). [I]
- Simple onboarding for a solo-to-20 crew shop, unlike LMN/Aspire/ServiceTitan learning curves. [I]
- Caveat: Apple-only limits crews with Android phones; many field users are Android (HCP Android discussion). Consider a lightweight web/companion for crew members. [I]
