## 3. Does our price actually win? (competitor monthly list price for N crews)

Assumptions: 1 crew = 1 paid user/route/driver; office staff are free for us, but count as paid seats at per-user vendors, so those totals are **understated** for Jobber, HCP, Kickserv, ReachOut and Upper. Competitor tier is the lowest one that fits N. Where a vendor sells fixed seat bundles, we use the smallest bundle that fits. "Mo" = month-to-month list; "Ann" = annual-billed list. Our price is monthly only, so vs annual figures we are not like-for-like.

Findings:
- **1 crew: we win.** Pro $29 is below every full field-service product with a public price, including Jobber Core annual ($29, a tie, but Core is $49 month-to-month). Exceptions are free or tiny plans: Yardbook free (unverified), Plow360 Solo $20 (25 sites), ReachOut Free/Standard, Workyard.
- **3 crews, Pro $79:** beats Jobber ($149-199), HCP Essentials ($149-189), GorillaDesk ($149+), Service Fusion ($220-259). Loses to **Kickserv ($60 for 5 users)**, Plow360 Team ($40, site-capped), Yeti ($66.50, 25 sites), and ReachOut per-user pricing.
- **5 crews, Pro $129:** still below Jobber Connect ($149 annual), HCP ($149), SF ($220) and GorillaDesk Basic ($209). Same losses as above, plus the gap to Kickserv widens.
- **10 crews, Pro $219:** margin collapses. Beats Jobber Connect annual by $10, Service Fusion annual by only **$1** ($220), and is above Kickserv Run ($119) and Plow360 Team/Pro. Against Service Fusion monthly ($259) we win by $40, and Service Fusion includes unlimited users.
- **Max:** 1 crew $79 beats Jobber Grow ($149-199), but at 3+ crews it loses to Jobber Grow annual at 5 ($229 vs our $235) and is far above at 10 ($299 annual / $399 monthly vs $380). It is level with HCP Max at 10 ($369-399) and above Service Fusion Starter/Plus annual ($220/$339). Max is cheaper than GorillaDesk Pro at every size ($99/$199/$299/$549).
- Our free office seats are a real edge only against per-user vendors (Jobber, HCP, Kickserv, ReachOut, Upper), not against Service Fusion, GorillaDesk or Aspire, which already give unlimited users.
- Payments: Service Fusion, LMN (LMN Pay), GorillaDesk (Square/Stripe) and Kickserv have payment features; we have none in v1. That cuts against us when features are compared.
- Trial: 14 days matches Jobber/HCP/GorillaDesk. Kickserv (30), OptimoRoute (30), and Yeti (60-day refund) are longer.

## 4. Recommended price adjustments

The weak point is the per-crew step, not the base. At 5+ crews the slope ($25 / $39) eats the advantage over Jobber/HCP annual rates and loses to Kickserv and snow tools.

1. **Pro extras: $25 to $19 (crews 2-5), $18 to $12 (6+).** Totals become 1/3/5/10 = $29 / $67 / $105 / $165. That is about $55 under Service Fusion annual at 10 crews and $44+ under Jobber Connect at 5.
2. **Max extras: $39 to $29 (2-5), $29 to $19 (6+).** Totals become $79 / $137 / $195 / $290. That undercuts Jobber Grow annual at 5 ($229) and at 10 ($299), and HCP Max at 10.
3. **Add a cheaper annual option** (about 20% off, as Jobber/Kickserv/GorillaDesk do) so headline prices are comparable: Pro 1 crew about $23.
4. **Do not expect to beat Kickserv, Plow360 or ReachOut on raw price at 3-10 crews.** Compete on features (routing, GPS and time tracking in Pro, snow features) and say so. Our Pro/Max feature lists are not in this file, so feature parity with Jobber Connect/Grow, HCP Essentials/Max and GorillaDesk Pro is **unchecked**.
5. For snow, Plow360 Pro ($140 from, with time clock, storm dispatch, QBO) is the closest like-for-like. Our Max at 1-3 crews ($79-157) is in the same range; at 5+ crews Max is above it. Plow360 is capped by site count and we are not, so state that.

- Route4Me prices are tagged valid until 2026-12-15; Black Friday promos (LMN to Nov 30, Yeti) distort headline pricing.
