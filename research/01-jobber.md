# Research 01: What users dislike about Jobber
Date: 2026-11-16. Labels: [SOURCED] = seen in a fetched page; [INFERENCE] = my judgment; [UNVERIFIED] = vendor claim / could not confirm.

## Method limits (read first)
- G2, Capterra, Software Advice returned HTTP 403 to fetch; I only saw search-result snippets of them. Reddit/Facebook/YouTube/App Store text was NOT reachable; no Reddit threads surfaced. Only Trustpilot text was fetched directly (20 recent 1-2 star reviews).
- Many "Jobber alternative" pages are competitor marketing (OneCrew, QuoteIQ, Aspire, Service Autopilot, Upper). Treated as [UNVERIFIED].
- So frequency rankings below are an impression from Trustpilot negatives + snippets, not a statistical count.

## 1. Pricing [SOURCED: https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/ fetched 2026-11-16]
Per month (no-commit / 1-yr commit / annual-prepaid):
- Core, 1 user: $49 / $39 / $29
- Connect, 1 user: $139 / $119 / $99; 5 users: $199/$169/$149; 10: $299/$259/$229; 15: $399/$339/$299
- Grow, 1 user: $199/$169/$149; 5: $299/$259/$229; 10: $399/$349/$299; 15: $499/$429/$399
- Plus, 5 users: $499/$439/$399; 10: $599/$499/$449; 15: $699/$599/$529
- Extra users $29/mo each; 16+ users or multi-location = contact sales.
- Add-ons: Marketing Suite $99/mo (a competitor page, OneCrew, says $79; official page said $99); AI Receptionist $29/mo (possible usage overage per OneCrew); Pipeline $49/mo. Plus bundles all three. Reviews, Campaigns, Request Management listed without prices; GPS tracking via fleet partners.
- Payments (same all plans): card 2.9% + 30c; Tap to Pay 2.7% + 30c; ACH (US) 1%; instant payout +1%. Runs on Stripe.
- Annual prepaid is non-refundable mid-term (OneCrew, competitor; consistent with Trustpilot cancellation complaints).
- Note: older sites still show stale prices (e.g. Core $39). Connect had a price increase in 2024 (https://www.getjobber.com/connect-price-increase-2024/). I found no verified 2025-26 backlash event.

## 2. Complaint themes (ranked by my impression of frequency)
Primary evidence: Trustpilot 1-2 star page https://www.trustpilot.com/review/getjobber.com?stars=1&stars=2 (reviews dated Mar-Nov 2026; Trustpilot overall ~3.9 across 143 reviews; G2 ~4.6/524 and Capterra ~4.6/1,420 per search snippets, so overall sentiment is positive).

1. Billing, cancellation, refunds, unwanted charges (very frequent in Trustpilot negatives). "They still refuse to allow the annual plan to be cancelled." (Nov 2026); "they reactivated my account without my approval" (Jul 2026); "I was charged the full month for not using the service." (May 2026); one reviewer alleges $1,000+ in charges (unverified claim).
2. Customer support quality/slowness. "Horrible and unhelpful customer service" (Oct 2026); "support that simply wasn't equipped to resolve our problems" (Nov 2026). Note Software Advice/Trustpilot snippets also say many praise support, so this is polarized.
3. Price vs value / feature paywalls. "The software at 180$ a month is already expensive enough." (Oct 2026); "the only solution offered is to upgrade" (Jun 2026); "Way too expensive if you aren't using 100% of the features offered" (Mar 2026).
4. Payments: holds, limits, payout trouble. "Jobber has literally zero customer service when it comes to your money." (May 2026). Jobber's own help pages confirm holds/processing limits exist (https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/jobber-payments-holds-and-reviews/). Anecdotes of $7,000 held ~120 days are single reports (unverified).
5. Frequent UI/layout changes. "they keep changing the layout on the screen" (Apr 2026); "A year ago it was so much easier to use" (May 2026); updates "most of which have little to no value" (Jul 2026).
6. Limited reporting. "Their reports options are extremely limited" (Aug 2026).
7. Accounting/integration sync problems. "The syncing between Jobber and QuickBooks is frustrating and unreliable." (Jun 2026, Trustpilot). Capterra snippet: QB Desktop difficulties; GetApp snippet: Google Calendar syncs only every 24h; Xero App Store review: no two-way sync. Also Google Business integration changing a profile; API duplicating quote line items (Aug 2026).
8. Permissions/roles too coarse. "there is no way to give limited access to an admin assistant" (Jun 2026).
9. Bugs/data loss. "Want to lose quotes and be forced to pay them money to restore them?" (Jun 2026); SoftwareFinder snippet: "very buggy", duplicate notifications, receipt errors after auto-payments.
10. Customization limits (G2 snippet: settings and card-processing options; scheduling and invoicing difficulties).
11. Industry-specific gaps (e.g., chemical logging for pest control, Aug 2026): shows "not built for my vertical" theme.
12. Per-user cost growth with crew size / add-on stacking (OneCrew, QuoteIQ: competitor claims, [UNVERIFIED] as user opinion but math is verifiable from pricing: 20-user Plus ~$844/mo before processing per OneCrew).

## 3. What users like (don't break) [SOURCED snippets: Capterra, GetApp, Trustpilot, Software Advice]
- Ease of use and clean mobile app; drag-and-drop calendar scheduling praised.
- Quote-to-invoice flow, client portal, online quote approval/digital signature, automated reminders.
- Support staff praised by many reviewers; Jobber replies to ~86% of negative Trustpilot reviews.
- Quick onboarding for small crews; broad integrations (QuickBooks, Stripe payments).

## 4. Snow/landscaping route-work gaps
- Route optimization: Jobber's feature page (https://www.getjobber.com/features/route-optimization/) [SOURCED] describes auto-routing for crews, set start/end points, multi-day/week planning, re-optimization on delay/cancel, turn-by-turn. It does NOT state limits on stops, priorities, or time-window constraints. Plan availability unclear (Upper Inc. claims Connect+; other vendors say Grow+; [UNVERIFIED]).
- No snow-specific features seen on pricing/feature pages: no storm-trigger mass dispatch, no priority tiers/service-level (e.g., commercial lots by trigger depth), no per-push/per-inch/seasonal contract billing rules, no saved recurring "snow event" route lists, no weather integration [mostly competitor-sourced absence claims, [UNVERIFIED]; absence on Jobber pages is [SOURCED] only for what I read].
- Old LawnSite thread (~12 yrs, https://www.lawnsite.com/threads/jobber-a-place-to-talk-about-it.418848/) wanted saved regenerable snow lists; dated, but shows long-standing need.
- GPS tracking is via fleet partners, not native depth; proof-of-service (timestamped photos, arrival/departure geofence logs, liability protection) not evidenced as a core strength.
- No property measurement built in (QuoteIQ claim, [UNVERIFIED]).
- Windows/offline, Mac/iPad-native experience: not researched.

## 5. Opportunity to beat them [INFERENCE]
1. Transparent, flat pricing: include users and marketing; no per-seat creep, no add-on stacking; easy monthly cancellation (billing/cancellation is the loudest Trustpilot theme and trust is cheap to win).
2. Snow-first workflow: storm triggers, priority-based dispatch and re-sequencing, seasonal/per-push/per-event billing, saved route templates per storm, subcontractor tracking, auto proof-of-service (geofenced timestamps + photos) as liability defense.
3. Native GPS time clock and crew tracking included, tied to payroll and job costing.
4. Payments: lower or transparent fees, clear hold policy and fast human support for money issues.
5. Rock-solid QuickBooks two-way sync, richer reports, granular roles.
6. Stability: stop gratuitous UI churn; ship a predictable, native Apple (iPhone/iPad/Mac) experience with offline mode.
7. Keep: simple quote-to-invoice, drag-and-drop calendar, client portal, friendly onboarding.
