# Owner Interview Script (15-20 minutes)

**Goal:** learn what snow and landscaping owners actually use, hate and would pay for. Don't pitch. Listen. Record (with permission) or take notes, and capture exact words.

**Who to talk to (aim for 10-15):** mix of solo operators, 2-5 crew shops and a few 10+ crew companies. At least 8 should do snow. Include some on Jobber, some on Housecall Pro/Aspire/LMN/Service Autopilot, and some on spreadsheets/QuickBooks/paper.

**Opening (1 min):** "I used to run a landscaping and snow business and I'm building a tool for owners like you. I'm not selling anything, I just want to hear how you run things. 15 minutes, okay if I take notes?"

## Section 1: Their business (2 min)
1. What do you do (snow, lawn, both), and how many crews/trucks/people?
2. Roughly how many accounts or sites in winter vs summer?
3. Contracts: per push, seasonal, per inch/trigger, hourly, or a mix? Which pays the most? Which causes the most headaches?

## Section 2: Tools today (4 min)
4. What do you use to schedule, dispatch, invoice and track time? (Walk through a storm night.)
5. What do you like about it? What makes you want to throw your phone?
6. Have you switched tools? What made you switch, and what almost stopped you?
7. What do you pay in total each month for software? Does it feel fair?
8. Have you ever been stuck trying to cancel something or been charged unexpectedly?

## Section 3: Storm night and proof (4 min)
9. Walk me through the last big storm: who decides to go, how crews find out, how you handle changes mid-storm.
10. How do you tell customers you were there? How often do you get "did you come?" calls?
11. Have you ever had a damage or slip-and-fall claim? What proof did you have? What did you wish you had?
12. How do you pay subs or drivers (percent, hourly, per push)? What goes wrong?

## Section 4: Routes and billing (3 min)
13. How do you decide the order of stops? Have you tried a route optimizer? What happened?
14. After a storm, how long does billing take and how do you do it?
15. What job in the office takes up the most time that shouldn't?

## Section 5: Fit and price (3 min)
16. Which phone do your crews use? Anyone on Android? Any iPad or Mac in the office?
17. If one tool did the things you just described at a fair price, what would you expect to pay for 1 crew? For 3?
18. What would make you switch, and what would make you never switch?
19. Would you want payments built in, or are you happy with your current way to get paid?

## Close (1 min)
20. "If I send you a TestFlight in a few months, would you try it for 14 days?" (Collect name, phone/email, only with permission.)
21. "Who else should I talk to?"

## After each interview (5 min)
Fill in one row in your tracking sheet:
- Date, name/business (private), snow/lawn/both, crews, current tool, monthly software spend
- Top 3 pains in their words
- Contract types
- iPhone / Android / both
- Price they said they'd pay (1 crew, 3 crews)
- Would try a trial? (Y/N/maybe)
- Surprises

## Rules
- Ask about past behavior ("last storm"), not hypotheticals.
- Don't mention our features first. Let them bring up problems.
- Don't promise features or dates. The founding-member payments promise is draft only until terms are settled.
- Keep personal details private. Don't share names or contact details outside the team.
