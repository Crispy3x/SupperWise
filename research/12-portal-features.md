## Must-haves (nearly everyone has)
All [E] or [S] as above.
1. Portal or link to pay invoices online by card or ACH (Jobber, HCP, Aspire, SingleOps, ServiceTitan).
2. Quote or estimate approval and a service-request path (Jobber, HCP, SingleOps, ServiceTitan).
3. Appointment reminders by text or email.
4. On-my-way or dispatch/arrival notification (Jobber, HCP, GorillaDesk, ServiceTitan, Aspire, Yeti).
5. Job-complete notification (Aspire, GorillaDesk, SingleOps, Yeti, ServiceTitan survey).
6. Overdue invoice reminders.
7. Photo and timestamp proof of service (Aspire, Plow360, Yeti).

## Ideas (one or two vendors only)
- Charge saved card automatically when a visit is completed (Jobber, recurring jobs only, plan-gated). This is the closest match to your auto-invoice-on-stop-complete and nobody else was confirmed.
- Client sees place in today's route by text sign-in (SnowBossPro, per a competitor's blog; unverified).
- Snow-depth trigger per site (Plow360, internal only).
- Client live-tracking link as a paid add-on (Upper Crew, OptimoRoute).
- Tip and referral from portal (Jobber, HCP).
- Card-expiry warnings before autopay (SingleOps).
- Weather integration (Plowdesk listing).

## Gaps nobody fills well for snow
All [I], based on "not found" results, so verify before claiming in marketing.
1. Per-push invoice automatically at stop completion, shown to the client with timestamp, photo and depth. Jobber's auto-charge is for recurring jobs and not push-aware. Aspire, GorillaDesk and Yeti pages did not mention per-push billing.
2. Pre-storm ETA window for each property. Only live-tracking add-ons from generic route tools came up.
3. Automatic storm-start messages driven by forecast or trigger depth. Only GoHighLevel and an unnamed AI tool were described, via a search summary of marketing pages, so unverified. No mainstream field-service tool was confirmed.
4. Storm status page in the portal (queued, in progress, done, skipped) and a "skip this storm" or "service request during storm" action. None confirmed.
5. Seasonal-contract view: pushes so far versus the cap or base fee, with a running bill. Not found.
6. Landscaping recurring-visit portal and snow in one client view. Jobber, Aspire and GorillaDesk cover both trades, but none was confirmed to unify them.

## Complaints found
- Jobber sent invoice reminders for cancelled jobs, and support said it was a feature: https://community.getjobber.com/discussions/invoicing-getting-paid/jobber-bug---invoice-reminders-for-cancelled-jobs/2174/replies/3709 [S, seen only in a search snippet; the page returned 403]. Lesson [I]: suppress reminders when a stop is cancelled or skipped.
- A Trustpilot summary mentions difficulty with automated reminders: https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/getjobber.com?page=6 [S, search summary only].
- Plan gating: Jobber's reminders and follow-ups are absent on the Core plan [E]. GorillaDesk invoice auto-send needs the Triggers add-on [S]. ServiceTitan texting needs SMS registration and carrier fees [S].
- Yardbook lacks a portal per Jobber's competitor page [S].
- No direct client-side complaints (spam, confusing portal) found.
