### Software they use / dislike
1. QuickBooks is the default billing tool but not an ops tool. "works good for fin. stuff but not running the operation." https://www.plowsite.com/threads/invoicing-software.80733/ (old, ~2007-2011)
2. Snow-specific billing tools praised for speed. A Blizzard Buster user: "do all my billing, start to finish, in about 20 - 30min after a storm." Another likes PlowTrax for entering service data for many clients at once. https://www.plowsite.com/threads/snowplow-billing-software.52563/ (also invoicing-software.80733)
3. Prior LawnPro user: "hated it, customer service sucked and the software was buggy." https://www.plowsite.com/threads/invoicing-software.80733/
4. Jobber, no bulk reschedule: on a rain day "You had to do it one by one." Poster called it a deal breaker (may have changed since). https://www.lawnsite.com/threads/jobber-a-place-to-talk-about-it.418848/
5. Jobber multi-crew: "scheduling is difficult for more than one crew"; one owner moved mow crews back to a spreadsheet as Jobber scheduling was too time-consuming. Another: the "move visits" action moves all team members' visits for that day; "doesnt help much when there are differet crews." Same thread as 4.
6. Jobber recurring routes: changing service day or crew "requires going in and editing each customer schedule. Very time consuming." Same thread.
7. Jobber invoices list every visit as its own line item (long invoices); auto-generation failed unless visit-based billing; many clicks per job to close and invoice; wish for more flexible due dates and late fees. https://www.lawnsite.com/threads/jobber-business-management-software-review.392517/ and thread 418848 above.
8. Jobber payments fee: "the only major problem with jobber is their payments service which % wise isn't competitive with quickbooks for a larger operation." Thread 418848.
9. Jobber cannot track drive time; an owner moved to Service Autopilot to "directly see how inefficient a route might be." https://www.lawnsite.com/threads/jobber-vs-service-autopilot.506532/ (search-listed; snippet via search summary)
10. Service Autopilot: complaints of rising cost, bugs, weak support, "code is over a decade old and is incapable of any meaningful integrations"; add-ons nickel-and-dime (client portal $20 extra); startup-fee haggling. https://www.lawnsite.com/threads/service-autopilot-users.513305/ , https://www.lawnsite.com/threads/has-anyone-had-luck-with-service-autopilot-waiving-the-startup-fee.498231/ . Capterra: bugs after updates, yearly price increases, QuickBooks sync issues, "templates are very confusing... we finally gave up and switched to LawnPro." https://www.capterra.com/p/122075/Service-Autopilot/reviews/ (several reviews 2016-2018)
11. Aspire/LMN (review sites): Aspire "very very expensive", steep learning curve, "some common tasks require too many steps", built for maintenance more than construction. LMN reviewer: "significantly more manual office administration than expected" for nearly $600/mo. Switchers go both directions (LMN to Aspire "generally good move"; others Aspire/SA to LMN for ease). https://www.capterra.com/p/161544/Aspire/reviews/ , https://www.capterra.com/p/142064/LMN/reviews/ (vendor-incentivized reviews possible)

### Billing / pricing / subs
12. Per-push vs per-inch confusion and custom rates: an operator with 85 contract customers asked how to handle fixed rate per snow amount; replies: price groups or separate invoices via payment service. https://www.plowsite.com/threads/invoicing-software.80733/ (page 2). Thread titled "Guys doing per push and per inch pricing, HOW THE HECK DO YOU PUSH THAT WAY?!?!?!" https://www.plowsite.com/threads/guys-doing-per-push-and-per-inch-pricing-how-the-heck-do-you-push-that-way.126035/ (title only; content unread)
13. Sub pay is a negotiated mess: "common to payout 50%-75%"; another paid subs "40-90% of the job"; per-job pay avoids caring about time. Subs complain of contractors holding funds "over 90 days." https://www.plowsite.com/threads/paying-a-sub.171284/ and https://www.plowsite.com/threads/rough-markup-on-subcontractors.63937/
14. Time-based sub pay can include non-plowing time, so logs matter in disputes (same threads).

### Proof of service
15. GPS on every truck "records closest address, arrival time, departure time, number of stops" as proof of service; another uses a clock-in app that geotags each job and records inches. https://www.plowsite.com/threads/any-apps-or-gps-units-that-track-time-in-out-of-places.169272/

(PlowSite slip-and-fall threads, e.g. https://www.plowsite.com/threads/slip-and-fall-claim.100929/ ; exact thread for the quote not confirmed.) ### Routing / dispatch

Route4me/Speedyroute need manual drag-and-drop. https://www.plowsite.com/threads/routing-software-or-route-planning.179923/ and https://www.plowsite.com/threads/looking-for-route-planning-software.164510/
18. Wish: app showing the next lot by GPS location; today dispatchers print lists and drivers type addresses into phone GPS. https://www.plowsite.com/threads/are-there-any-apps-or-gps-units-that-track-time-in-out-of-places.169272/ is not this one; source thread was in the routing results (https://www.plowsite.com/threads/are-there-any-apps-for-phone-or-tablet-to-organize-a-plow-route.165236/), attribution approximate.
19. Follosoft (commercial/larger fleets) praised for geofenced auto-arrival: "when the driver enters the driveway, it sends a text notification to the 10th house down the list." Recommended at 50-60+ stops or frequent changes. https://www.plowsite.com/threads/routing-software-app.169371/

That is 19 items, but items 5/6/7/8 share one LawnSite mega-thread and several attributions are approximate; treat as about 15 independent voices.

## Frequency impression
- Recurring: multi-crew scheduling and bulk rescheduling weakness (Jobber); per-visit invoice clutter; software price creep and add-on fees (SA, Aspire, LMN); learning curve/admin burden of the big landscape platforms; distrust of auto-routing; GPS/log proof of service valued.
- One-offs: late-fee flexibility, drive-time tracking, geofence notifications.

## Contradicts or complicates our assumptions
- Operators rarely wish for "conditions-aware" optimization. Many distrust computer routing and hand-check routes. Pitch it as dispatcher-controlled re-sequencing, not an autopilot.
- Many snow operators are content with QuickBooks plus a snow-specific billing tool; the speed of post-storm billing (20-30 min) is the bar to beat.
- Proof of service is already wanted and low-tech (GPS trackers, clock-in app); value is insurer/claim defense, not marketing.
- Sub pay is mostly percentage-of-job, negotiated ad hoc; sub-payment automation is a wish I did not see stated explicitly.
- Marketing, CRM breadth and "all-in-one" were not mentioned by operators in anything I saw; complaints centre on scheduling, billing, price.
- Evidence skews old and lawn-heavy; I found no Apple-vs-Android or "Apple-native" sentiment.
